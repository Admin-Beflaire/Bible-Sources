**Last updated: 12 October 2026.** These terms describe use of Canonical Scripture Study, maintained by **Mark Gatzen**.

## The service

Canonical Scripture Study provides read-only tools and instructions for studying biblical texts in ChatGPT. It is free, with no purchases, payment processing or paid plugin features. OpenAI's own account requirements, charges and terms apply independently.

The public endpoint is restricted to OpenAI's connector network and has shared rate limits. Availability depends on that network, Cloudflare hosting and the chat host. The maintainer may change, suspend or discontinue the service, including to address security problems, source issues or hosting limits. No availability or support response-time guarantee is offered.

## Study and interpretation

The plugin is offered as a study aid within a Christian biblical and canonical framework. Working translations, explanations and theological conclusions are generated by the host model using retrieved evidence and instructions. They may contain errors or disputed judgments. They are not an official church statement, certified translation or substitute for examining the biblical text.

Source coverage and methodological limits should remain visible. A missing source record is not proof that a manuscript omitted a passage, and a structured validation check does not establish linguistic or theological truth.

## Responsible use

Use the service for its supported study tasks. Do not attempt to bypass the network restrictions, defeat rate limits, obtain private credentials, alter source records or interfere with the service. The tools cannot save or publish a study, send messages or perform transactions. You may disconnect or stop using the plugin at any time.
