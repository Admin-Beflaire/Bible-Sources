Canonical Scripture Study is a free Bible-study plugin by **Mark Gatzen**, prepared for public availability in ChatGPT. Its public directory review has not yet been completed.

## Study Scripture in conversation

Ask for a passage study, a working translation, a word study, literary or historical context, or a follow-up question. The plugin retrieves identified Hebrew and Greek source material; ChatGPT translates and explains that evidence using the study instructions.

The approach follows the immediate context and the Bible's wider canonical story within a Christian framework. It distinguishes a passage's explicit statements from contextual inference and theological synthesis. Traditional and scholarly readings can be compared, and the reader's intended argument should be understood before its reasoning is corrected.

## Examples

- “Study Genesis 1.” The study includes Genesis 1:1–2:3 and explains why the seventh day completes the account.
- “Produce a working translation of Genesis 1:1–3 and John 1:1–3.” Source-supported wording connections and consequential alternatives receive notes.
- “What does Philippians 4:13 mean?” Follow-up questions can develop the argument without restarting a full study.
- “Full word study ruach.” The study distinguishes lexical meanings, contextual use and canonical interpretation.
- “Detailed study Romans 12:1–2, in Dutch.” English is the default; another language can be requested.

## Access and scope

The plugin is free and has no purchases or additional user sign-in. The public MCP endpoint is `https://mcp.canonical-theology.com/mcp`, restricted to OpenAI's connector network. The intended initial directory availability is the European Union; this listing choice does not make the endpoint itself a country-based firewall. Rate limits protect the shared service.

The eleven hosted tools are read-only. They cannot edit the Bible source repository, save a personal notebook, send messages, make purchases or publish studies. The server stores no conversations or user accounts. ChatGPT's own account, availability and service terms apply separately.

## Sources and limitations

Source material includes WLC/OSHB, TAHOT, UXLC, Brenton's Greek Septuagint, SBLGNT and its edition-comparison apparatus, selected lexical records, curated word studies and indexed Dead Sea Scroll material. Returned evidence preserves edition identity and provenance. Coverage is selective: this is not a complete manuscript apparatus, exhaustive lexicon or automatically reviewed cross-edition collation.

Translations and explanations are generated by the host model and can be mistaken. Check consequential claims against the cited sources. The study framework and curated material are inspectable in the [Bible-Sources repository](https://github.com/Admin-Beflaire/Bible-Sources).
